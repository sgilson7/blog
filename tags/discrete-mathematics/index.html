  • 2 October 2026 — Thinking about curriculum as a knowledge graph I want to explore how a curriculum might help students build connections between knowledge components, and how working through concrete problems might make those connections easier to draw. Starting with cats and multisets, I work through a graph account of remembering and an experience connecting interpolation to recurrence relations. These are provisional ideas that need a literature review, including work that could contradict them, before I use them to design a combinatorics unit.
